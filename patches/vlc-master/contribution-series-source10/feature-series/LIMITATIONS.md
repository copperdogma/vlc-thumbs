## Product support boundary

Only nonempty regular local files qualify, including mounted NAS files; URLs,
secondary resources and helper network protocols do not. Exactly one video must
be selected. A sole video maps directly; multi-video flat AVC Matroska needs an
exact, unique representable TrackNumber. Ordered/linked timelines, unsupported
mapping, unknown-size nested elements, invalid bounds and exhausted admission
budgets fail closed. Some normally playable files therefore have no preview.

Caches are bounded to 32 MiB RAM/256 MiB disk. Fresh reopen requalifies metadata
plus sampled SHA256; unsampled changes can escape detection. Provisional RAM
images share one 15-second Checking source deadline that pointer activity cannot
renew; change, failure or expiry clears them. Protocol deadlines do not guarantee
hard timing for stalled kernel IO/process reap. Cold/stalled/larger NAS sources,
universal latency and exhaustive formats remain unqualified.

Exposed media position has shown stale/inaccurate values in both baseline and
candidate comparisons. This contribution does not repair position accuracy or
qualify physical precision seeking. Historical settings/cache/source-identity and
paused foreground knob-response observations retain their narrow source-lineage
scope, not blanket current playback, resource, fade or lifecycle equivalence.
