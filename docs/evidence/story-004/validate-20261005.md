**P2 — Wrong selected-track preview when VLC and libav discover different video counts.**

**P2 — Background disk pruning can evict a recently hovered RAM thumbnail.**
`src/macosx/VLCThumbnailCache.m:141` refreshes the payload's disk modification time only on disk reads; a RAM hit updates only process-local recency. Disk pruning at lines 219–222 orders files by their modification time. The isolated Cocoa reproduction sets an existing hot payload's mtime to 1, retrieves it from RAM, fills the logical disk quota with a newer cold sparse entry, then calls background `storeRaw` with `allowEviction:NO`. The hot payload is removed, still works from RAM, and misses after reopening. `work/validation/runtime-review-lru/result.json` records `ram_hit=memory`, `mtime_after_ram_hit=1`, `hot_payload_retained_after_background_store=false`, and `restart_hit=false`. The sparse fixture was removed; production caches were untouched. Protect useful resident/hovered samples during background pruning and make meaningful RAM use count toward disk retention, without treating passive manifest enumeration as a hover. Add a quota-pressure regression.
