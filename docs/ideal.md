# VLC timeline enhancements — Ideal v0

## Product Ideal

Cam opens a video in VLC and can immediately see what happens anywhere along
its timeline. Moving through the timeline feels like looking through the video:
the right image is there at the right moment, with no waiting or preparation.
Exploring another moment leaves the current viewing experience undisturbed.

A moment worth remembering is easy to mark with a brief label in Cam's own
words.
Those remembered moments are visible whenever the timeline appears. Each note
clearly belongs to its moment; reading it or returning to that moment is
effortless. Notes are easy to correct or remove.

Whenever Cam returns to a video, its remembered moments are already there.
There is nothing to save separately, organize, reconnect, or remember to enable.
Notes stay with the right video, and the original video stays intact. Videos
and personal notes remain private by default.

This feels like a natural part of watching in VLC. Finding, remembering, and
revisiting moments is immediate and dependable, while familiar playback controls
remain comfortable and accessible.

## Execution Ideal

Cam describes the experience they want, receives a complete working result,
and refines it through conversation. Trying the result makes decisions easy.
The effort goes into shaping the experience; technical work happens smoothly
in the background. What works and what still needs refinement are clear.

## Vision-Level Preferences

The experience is immediate, unobtrusive, and part of the player. Remembered
moments belong to their videos. Original media and personal privacy are respected.
Accessible use and dependable playback remain essential as the product evolves.

## Requirements

- **ideal:req:previews:** See the corresponding moment immediately while exploring the timeline, without interrupting current viewing.
- **ideal:req:annotations:** Attach brief personal labels to any moment and discover those notes in context whenever the timeline appears.
- **ideal:req:persistence:** Remembered moments are already present whenever the same video is revisited, without separate saving or organization.
- **ideal:req:navigation:** Return effortlessly to a remembered moment, and correct or remove its note when desired.
- **ideal:req:media-integrity:** Original videos stay intact, and videos and personal notes remain private by default.
- **ideal:req:playback-quality:** Exploring and remembering moments preserves comfortable, accessible playback and control.
- **ideal:req:evidence:** The experience is dependable, and what the product can actually do is clear and trustworthy.

## Minimum Viable Floor

Both seeing moments and remembering them work together in VLC's timeline.
Cam can explore a video, annotate a moment, leave, and return to find that note
and revisit its moment. Both capabilities feel like part of the same viewing
experience.

## Quality Bar / Root Outcome

Given a video, Cam can immediately recognize any moment, remember it in their
own words, and reliably find it again later. This remains easy while watching,
with intact media, private notes, and comfortable control. That complete
experience is the North Star; today's delivery limits and proof procedures
belong in the spec and eval contracts.
